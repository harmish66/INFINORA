Privacy Policy for Infinora: Infinite Canvas

Effective Date: November 19, 2026 | Last Updated: November 19, 2026

Core Privacy Principle: Infinora is built with a local-first architecture. Your drawings, sketches, canvas layers, notes, and imported photos remain stored strictly on your personal device. We do not operate external creative servers or cloud storage for your artwork. The app is 100% free with no in-app purchases.

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy describes how Infinora: Infinite Canvas ("we", "us", or "our") handles user information when you use our mobile application (the "App") available on the Google Play Store (Package Name: com.infinora.studio.infinora).

By downloading, installing, or using Infinora, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please do not use the application.

2. Artwork and Creative Content (Local Storage Only)

Infinora operates primarily as an offline-capable digital art and sketching application:

  • Zero Cloud Upload of Artwork: All vector paths, pencil strokes, painted layers, canvas coordinates, and exported project files are written directly to your device local storage and private SQLite database.
  • No Account Required: You do not need to create an account, register an email address, or sign in to use the canvas features.
  • Image Import: When you import a picture into your canvas, that image is processed locally on your device and is never uploaded to any external server.

3. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

To support ongoing maintenance and free access to all canvas tools, the App displays advertisements using Google AdMob (operated by Google LLC). Google AdMob may collect and process certain device and usage data to serve ads, measure ad performance, and prevent fraud:

  • Advertising ID (such as Google Advertising ID / AAID on Android)
  • Coarse location data derived from IP address (country or city level, no precise GPS tracking)
  • Device information (device model, operating system version, screen resolution, carrier)
  • Ad interaction metrics (impressions, clicks, ad format performance)

For more details on how Google processes and protects advertising data, please review:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use their services
  • Google AdMob Privacy and Security Information

Opting Out of Personalized Ads: You can reset or opt out of personalized advertising at any time on your Android device by going to Settings > Google > Ads and enabling "Delete advertising ID" or "Opt out of Ads Personalization".

4. No In-App Purchases or Financial Transactions

Infinora does not provide, offer, or process any in-app purchases, paid subscriptions, paywalls, or digital commerce features. All drawing, sketching, painting, infinite zoom, and canvas export features are provided completely free of charge. We do not collect, process, or store credit card details, debit card numbers, billing addresses, or any banking or financial information.

5. Device Permissions

The App requests only standard runtime permissions necessary to provide user-initiated creative features:

  • Photos / Media Access (Photo Picker or READ_MEDIA_IMAGES): Used only when you tap the photo icon to import a reference image into your canvas, or when you choose to export your artwork as PNG, JPEG, or PDF to your device photo gallery. The App never scans or accesses media files outside your explicit selection.
  • Internet Access (android.permission.INTERNET): Required to load advertisements through Google AdMob and load external policy links.
  • Network State (android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE): Used to verify active network connectivity before requesting ad banners.

6. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Families Policy)

Infinora is a general audience creative studio application designed for artists, designers, and students. The App is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in certain jurisdictions within the European Economic Area). We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under 13.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe that your child has provided personal information to third-party ad networks through the App, please contact us at the email listed below so we can take appropriate steps.

7. Data Retention and User Data Deletion

Because Infinora does not store your artwork or personal data on remote servers:

  • Artwork Deletion: Deleting a project or canvas within the App permanently removes that project file and associated database records from your device.
  • Full Data Purge: You can delete all local application data, cache, and canvas databases at any time by going to your device Settings > Apps > Infinora > Storage > Clear Storage / Clear Data, or by uninstalling the application.
  • Third-Party Ad Data: Advertising data collected by Google AdMob is managed according to Google retention schedules. You can reset your device Advertising ID in your device settings.

8. Data Security

The security of your data is paramount. All creative files are stored within the secure Android application sandbox, which prevents other third-party applications on your device from accessing your canvas files without authorization. Network communication for advertisements uses industry-standard TLS/HTTPS encryption.

9. California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA) and GDPR Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights regarding your personal data:

  • The right to know what personal information is collected by third-party ad networks.
  • The right to request deletion of personal information held by third-party providers.
  • The right to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information for targeted advertising through your device Google Ads settings.
  • The right to non-discrimination for exercising your privacy rights.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our legal obligations or application features. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
